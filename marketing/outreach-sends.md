## A · NZ Cycling Journal (send first, local angle)

**Subject:** Wellington dads build an aero app

Hi [name],

Two Wellington dads, with day jobs and kids, built an iPhone app called Get Tucked. It measures
the frontal area of a rider and their loaded bike from a photo, and it answers the question
bikepackers ask most: what do these bags cost me? Kiwi ultra riders are the people we built it
for. The app also tells you what a photo can't say about drag, in a category full of aero claims
nobody can check.

Each photo scales off two rulers already on the bike: the handlebar width, checked against the
wheel size. If those disagree, the app flags the photo. Each reading carries a fixed ±3% margin,
and below that floor the app calls two setups indistinguishable. The full method, with its
limits: https://impreciselyaccurate.com/get-tucked/methodology.html
