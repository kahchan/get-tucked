**Subject:** A Wellington-built app that measures your loaded rig's frontal area

Hi Joe,

I'm Kah, a fellow Wellingtonian. I've followed you from the Tour Divide podium to the Silk Road
win, and your Velo interview about the bikepacking aero hack told me you'd have opinions about
what we built.

A mate and I made an iPhone app called Get Tucked. You photograph yourself and your loaded bike
against a plain wall. The app segments the whole rig, fork bags and seatpack included, and gives
you frontal area in cm² plus posture angles. Then you compare setups: bags on against bags off,
hoods against drops.

Each photo scales off your handlebar width, and the app checks that against your wheel size, so a
bad tap shows up instead of skewing the number. Get Tucked won't predict CdA. We're two dads in
Wellington with day jobs and no wind tunnel, so it measures what a photo can measure and tells you
when two setups sit inside the noise.
