## 1 — Joe Nation `researched` → draft

**Subject:** A Kiwi-built frontal-area app — want it before launch?

Hi Joe — fellow Wellingtonian here, and I've followed your line from the Tour Divide podium to
the Silk Road win. But it was the piece on your bikepacking aero hack that made me sure you'd
have opinions about what we built.

A mate and I made an iPhone app that measures the frontal area of you *and* your loaded bike from
photos — fork bags, seatpack, position, all of it. You shoot against a plain wall, it segments the
whole rig, gives you cm² plus posture angles, and compares setups: bags on vs off, hoods vs drops.
It scales off your handlebar width and cross-checks that against your wheel size, so a bad tap gets
caught instead of quietly poisoning the number. It doesn't predict CdA and doesn't pretend to —
we're two dads in Wellington, about as far from a wind tunnel as you can get — it measures what a
photo can honestly measure, and tells you when a difference is inside your own noise.

It's called Get Tucked. Launching at USD $20, no subscription. I'd love to get it on your phone
before launch — no obligation to say anything about it, ever. If it's useful, use it.
