SHORT DESCRIPTION (50 words)
Get Tucked is an iPhone app that measures the frontal area of a cyclist and their loaded bike
from one photo against a plain wall. Riders compare positions and bag setups in cm². Each
reading carries an error bar, and the app says "indistinguishable" when a difference is too
small to trust.

LONG DESCRIPTION
Cyclists spend a lot on aero gear, and the usual ways to measure it are wind tunnels and
dedicated sensors. Get Tucked uses a phone photo. The rider stands on the bike in front of a
plain wall, a friend takes a head-on photo from 5 to 6 m back, and the rider taps the two ends
of the handlebar. The app segments rider, bike and bags on the phone and reports frontal area
in cm². Shoot a second setup, such as bags on against bags off or hoods against drops, and the
app compares the two.

HOW IT WORKS
- The handlebar width is the ruler. The rider enters it once, and it turns pixels into cm.
- The wheel is a second ruler. If the two rulers disagree, the app flags the photo.
- Each reading shows its error bar, at least ±3%. If two setups differ by less than their
  combined error, the app says "indistinguishable".

WHAT IT DOES NOT CLAIM
- It measures frontal area, not drag. It does not estimate CdA or model airflow.
- Its time estimate is a range, not a promise.

PRIVACY
Everything runs on the phone. No account, no analytics, no tracking, no network calls.
Photos never leave the phone.

ABOUT THE MAKERS
