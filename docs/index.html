Before you ask

The honest FAQ.

What do I need?

An iPhone on iOS 17 or later, and a plain, evenly lit wall. You enter your handlebar width once. That's your ruler.

How long does it take?

About a minute per shot. Take the photo, tap the two bar ends and the wheel, read the number.

Is there an Android version?

No. Get Tucked is native iOS. The segmentation runs on Apple's on-device vision, so nothing ever leaves your phone. No Android today.

Do I need a wind tunnel?

No. A phone and a wall. The one rule is a clean, evenly lit background. That's the difference between a sharp silhouette and a guess.

How accurate is it?

Every reading carries a ±3% margin, and the app tells you when a difference is smaller than that. It answers “did this change anything?” better than “what's my exact number?” Read the method.

What does it cost?

$20, once. No subscription, no account, no data collection. Works offline.
