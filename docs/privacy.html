Privacy policy

Get Tucked collects no data. Everything you photograph and measure stays on your phone. Last updated 15 October 2026.

What stays on your phone

Your photos, masks, measurements and bike details are stored on your device only. We have no server. The app makes no network calls, so nothing can be sent anywhere.

  • No accounts. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • No analytics or tracking. We do not count taps, sessions or users.
  • No third-party SDKs. The app uses only Apple frameworks.
  • No data collected. This matches the "Data Not Collected" label on the App Store.

What each permission is for

  • Camera. To photograph your riding position. The photo is analysed on the phone.
  • Photo library. To read a photo you pick as the source for a measurement, to read back captures the app saved, and to save source photos to your library. The app reads only the photos you pick.
  • Motion. To keep the camera level during capture. Motion readings are used live and are not stored.

None of this leaves your phone.

Local storage

The app keeps your settings on the device. It also checks free storage space before it saves a capture, so it can warn you when the phone is full. Neither is shared with anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete a position in the app to remove its photo and measurements. Delete the app to remove everything.
