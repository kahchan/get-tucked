Methodology
How the number is made.
01
Isolate
We separate you and the bike from everything behind you. The background is thrown away — only your silhouette survives.
02
Scale
Your handlebar width is a known ruler in the frame. It converts pixels into real centimetres — no depth sensor required.
03
Project
We sum the lit silhouette into one figure: your frontal area, in cm² — the surface the wind actually sees.
WHAT THE NUMBER IS — AND ISN'T
IT IS
+
A repeatable proxy for drag
+
Sensitive to position, not weather
+
Comparable shot to shot
IT ISN'T
−
A wind-tunnel CdA figure
−
A read on surface or yaw drag
−
A watts-saved promise
THE NOISE FLOOR
Every reading carries a ±2.1% margin. Re-shoot the same position to confirm a change is real — not measurement jitter.
